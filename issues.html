Issues & Priorities

Rooted in Dixon. Focused on Our Future. Working for You.

Safe Roads and Infrastructure

Advocate for safer roads, sidewalks, and pathways for pedestrians and children walking or bicycling to school. Support infrastructure improvements that strengthen emergency preparedness and address the challenges of a community divided by railroad tracks.

Responsible Growth and Community Input

Ensure Dixon residents have a meaningful voice in large-scale development decisions. Promote thoughtful planning that addresses increased traffic, public safety, and infrastructure needs while respecting the community’s small-town character and way of life.

Learn more about Keep Dixon Green!

Protect Farmland and Quality of Life

Protect Dixon’s farmland, open spaces, and natural habitats while preserving the peaceful character of the community. Pursue additional funding opportunities to help meet local needs and support a healthy quality of life for current and future residents.
